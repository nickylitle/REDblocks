Findings

  • Square hit at 12000 rpm: peak tooth force 47.1 kN, peak shaft load 19.6 kN, permanent bend 0.139 mm.
  • Shaft plastic hinge: Mp = 148 N m on the 10 mm 4140 Q&T ~30 HRC (prehard) shaft (collapse load 4 Mp / L = 19.8 kN; first yield at 97 N m).
  • Nominal hit PUNCHES THROUGH the 4 mm AR500 (indentation 4.0 mm).
  • 1 J goes into the opponent, 167 J into the contact dent, 2.5 J into bending the shaft. Upper bound from the rigid-body anchor (spinner_strike, e = 0, no punch-through): 561 J absorbed in the collision.
  • Monte Carlo (150 hits): P(bend > 0.1 mm) = 67%; top driver tooth corner radius (-0.89).
  • 4140 prehard diameters that stay under 0.1 mm: 12, 14 mm. The corrected 4140 prehard yield (890 vs the old 655 MPa) is what separates it from 4140 normalized.

Assumptions

  • Square hit: contact force perpendicular to the shaft at the blade's mid-span; blade on bearings.
  • Shaft simply supported between frame plates 30 mm apart (real plates add some end fixity).
  • Plastic hinge length = shaft diameter; 4140 prehard curve is the flagged Ramberg-Osgood fallback (no published fit for this temper).
  • Tooth contact: flat-punch stiffness at the 2 mm corner radius, then fully plastic indentation of the softer side (3Y, Tabor / Johnson ch. 6).
  • Opponent free (1.36 kg), attacker chassis free; no friction, no rotation of either chassis.
  • Opponent armor 4 mm AR500: once the tooth has indented through the thickness the plate is treated as punched out and contact ends (no petalling or plug-shear model yet).
