Findings

  • 95% of 12,000 rpm in 1.80 s; ideal (no sag, no losses) 1.17 s, so pack sag and drag cost 0.64 s.
  • Pack bottoms at 2.37 V/cell at the 60 A current limit; 25 mAh used in 3 s.
  • Windage absorbs 583 J and winding copper 177 J; pack internal loss 451 J.
  • Monte Carlo (200 runs): P(slow) = 46%, P(sag) = 100%. Top driver of spin-up time: pack resistance (rank corr -0.84).
  • Belt ratios 1.75, 2 never reach 95% speed on the nominal pack (gaps in the sweep): top speed is capped by KV x volts / ratio under sag and windage. A tired pack (0.25 ohm) never reaches it at any ratio tried.

Assumptions

  • Motor 1100 KV, 35 mOhm, 60 A ESC limit, 1.5:1 belt: ASSUMED (no motor named).
  • Pack 5S 450 mAh: R0 0.15 ohm (30 mOhm/cell, hobby IR band), R1 0.05 ohm, C1 600 F: ASSUMED.
  • LiPo OCV(SOC) table is an approximate typical curve (lipo_ocv block, unsourced).
  • Lossless ESC, full throttle from rest, no regen; belt losses ignored.
  • Windage: bar spinner, Cd 1.28, 6.35 mm thick bar.
