Assumptions

  • Weapon: two 2807 1350 KV motors (R 45-58 mOhm line-line from Axisflying, Hobbywing and GEPRC data; no-load current 1-2 A), each on its own current-limited ESC, friction-driving the blade at ratio 1.25-1.5.
  • Blade held at 12,000 rpm by throttle after each spin-up; windage from strip theory with an edge Cd of 0.05-0.4 (the air co-rotates with a bar spinning in its own plane, so the flat-plate 1.28 overstates it).

  • Pack: 6 cells, resistance per 450 mAh cell 8-25 mOhm scaled with 1/capacity, R1 = 0.5 R0, 20 s recovery; generic LiPo OCV curve (3.3-4.2 V). HV packs (4.35 V) would add about 3% energy.
  • Drive and weapon on the same 6S pack. Your notes say the drive ran on 5S before.
